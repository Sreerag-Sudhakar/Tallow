Built for the weeks that go sideways

Most habit apps are designed for your best week. Tallow is designed for the week a deadline lands on a Tuesday and the gym doesn't happen.

  • A missed day dims your streak. It doesn’t erase it.

    Other trackers drop you to zero the first time you slip. Tallow keeps the count and marks the gap, so one late night at work doesn't undo three good weeks. Miss two in a row and it asks, gently, whether the habit still fits your life.

  • Check in faster than you can unlock your phone

    One tap on the home-screen widget. Most check-ins take under ten seconds, so logging never becomes its own chore.

  • One reminder, at the hour you choose

    No badges, no confetti, no 6 a.m. guilt trip. Just a quiet note when you're usually free.

  • A Sunday look-back

    A short summary shows what held and what slipped, so you can adjust before a bad week turns into a bad month.

  • Your habits stay yours

    No public profiles, no leaderboards. Sync is end-to-end encrypted, and nothing gets shared unless you share it.

One habit, three steps

  1. Pick one habit

    Not five. One. Tallow asks what you want to do, and what the smallest version of it looks like on a bad day.

  2. Check in each evening

    Tap once when it’s done. If the evening gets away from you, you have until noon the next day to log it.

  3. Let a missed day pass

    The day shows as a gap, the streak carries on, and you won’t get a single guilt-trip notification about it.
